Student Café+

Privacy Policy

Overview

Student Café+ is an unofficial browser extension that changes the look and layout of the Chevalier College Student Cafe portal. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Chevalier College. We respect your privacy: the extension does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

Information the extension accesses

While you are on Student Cafe, the extension reads parts of the page, such as your displayed name, timetable and notices. It does this only to restyle and display the page on your device. This information is never sent to the developer or any third party.

Information stored on your device

The extension saves the following locally in your browser, using localStorage and Chrome extension storage:

  • Appearance settings (light/dark mode, accent colour, theme)
  • Subject names and links you add
  • A cached copy of the latest timetable banner message

This data stays on your device and is never uploaded. You can remove it at any time by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling the extension.

Network requests

The extension makes read-only requests to:

  • Cloudflare Workers operated by the developer, to check for announcements, offline notices and timetable banner messages
  • The Chevalier College website, to load the school logo

These requests don’t include your name, login details or any other personal data. As with any web request, the servers receiving them may see standard technical information such as your IP address. Cloudflare may process this under its own privacy policy.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect, store or transmit your login credentials, grades, attendance, payment details or other portal data.
  • We don’t use analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising.
  • We don’t sell or share data with third parties.
  • We don’t execute remotely hosted code. Content from our servers is displayed as plain text only.

Third-party links

The sidebar and subject links may open external sites (for example Canva or links you add yourself). Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

The extension is used by students, but it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions or bug reports: aw101007@students.chevalier.nsw.edu.au. This extension was made by a student. Extension not for public use.
