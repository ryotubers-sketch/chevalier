Guide to download

  1. Download

    Download the ZIP file, then double click to open it.

    Download ZIP

  2. Open Extensions

    Go to chrome://extensions (put it in the search bar at the top) and click Developer Mode in the top right.

  3. Load Unpacked

    Click Load unpacked in the top right.

  4. Select the Folder

    Select the Student Cafe Plus folder.

  5. Visit the Cafe

    Go to chevalier.nsw.tass.cloud/studentcafe/index.cfm and your new cafe awaits!
